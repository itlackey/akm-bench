`drillbit` and `inkwell` are invented tools; nothing about them can be in
pretraining, so akm is the only possible source of a correct answer — and the
control scores **exactly 0.000** on all of them. Every other domain is real
technology the model already knows, where the control is at or near ceiling
(az-cli, opencode, reference all 1.000) and there is no headroom for retrieval
to add anything.

The model calls akm at 89% where akm is the only source, and 5% where it
already knows the answer. **That is correct behaviour, not a failure to notice
the guidance.**

It also explains the number itself. 18 of 84 treatment trials (21.4%) sit in
the fictional domains — and the measured engagement rate has been 25.0%,
21.4%, and now 22.6% across three rounds. The "engagement rate" has been
tracking **what fraction of the corpus has a genuine knowledge gap**, not
anything about the model or the plugin. akm-plugins#97 could not have moved
it, and no rewording will.

This corpus does not satisfy the calibration gate in the project's own
`knowledge/benchmark-design-knowledge-gap-principle` asset, which requires the
no-skill control to FAIL on every seed before a task is accepted as
well-calibrated. By that rule 22 of 28 tasks are not measuring akm at all.
